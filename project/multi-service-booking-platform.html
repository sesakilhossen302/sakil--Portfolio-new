Multi-Service Booking Platform

A simple and user-friendly service booking platform that connects customers with professional service providers. Customers can discover and book a wide range of professional services, including doctors, teachers, lawyers, and other service providers. Service providers can manage their services and booking requests, while customers can select a required service, choose a suitable provider, and schedule a booking based on their needs. The platform is designed to make professional service discovery and booking simple, fast, and convenient.

Project Deep Dive & Architecture

Multi-Service Booking Platform was engineered from the ground up using Flutter & Dart, adhering strictly to Clean Architecture principles. Designed to deliver smooth 60 FPS performance, offline-first reliability, and seamless platform-specific adaptations for both iOS and Android.

Features include structured state management, responsive UI adaptation using ScreenUtil, real-time client-server communication, and military-grade data validation.
